    Settings and offline use

    While online, prepare OCR and wait for confirmation. Then test in airplane mode. iOS may remove cached files; reconnect and prepare again if needed.

    On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. The phone controls the camera interface and may offer photo selection options.

    Privacy and recognition limits

    Use a close, sharp photo. OCR proposes text for review; it can make mistakes. Select the state manually. Records are saved only after confirmation.

    Voice uses your browser’s speech service, which may send audio to its provider and require internet. Voice is optional and never saves a record automatically.

    Photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded or stored by this app. History is saved locally. Export CSV regularly: clearing browser data or losing your device can erase records. Safari and the installed app may have separate storage.
